     1→# NOMOFEE — COMPLETE AFFILIATE HUB & ONE-LEVEL SPONSOR REFERRAL SYSTEM
     2→## Master Architecture, Audit, Implementation & Verification Instruction
     3→
     4→### 1. MISSION
     5→
     6→Build a complete Affiliate System and Affiliate Hub for NOMOFEE.
     7→
     8→NOMOFEE is a multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace combining marketplace-style product discovery and checkout with independent seller storefronts.
     9→
    10→The objective is to allow ordinary registered users to become affiliates without needing to own a seller store, maintain inventory, purchase products, handle fulfilment or operate their own e-commerce business.
    11→
    12→Affiliates should be able to discover participating seller products, generate promotional links, share them externally through social media and messaging platforms, introduce new affiliates to NOMOFEE, and earn commissions from eligible customer purchases.
    13→
    14→The system must support the following commercial model:
    15→
    16→1. Seller-funded product affiliate commissions.
    17→2. Unlimited direct affiliate referrals.
    18→3. One direct sponsor per affiliate, at most.
    19→4. A fixed 90% / 10% commission allocation:
    20→   - 90% to the affiliate directly attributed to the eligible product sale.
    21→   - 10% to that affiliate's immediate direct sponsor.
    22→5. No commission beyond the immediate sponsor.
    23→6. No rewards for registration, recruitment, clicks, account creation or merely introducing another person.
    24→7. All affiliate commission liabilities must originate from genuine eligible product sales.
    25→
    26→Build this as a proper, integrated NOMOFEE subsystem, not a standalone demonstration or a collection of hardcoded screens.
    27→
    28→The implementation must integrate correctly with the existing:
    29→- User authentication and account system.
    30→- Seller and store authorization.
    31→- Product and product-variant structure.
    32→- Cart and multi-store checkout.
    33→- Order and OrderItem records.
    34→- Payment confirmation and idempotency.
    35→- Inventory and fulfilment lifecycle.
    36→- Seller settlement.
    37→- Wallet and financial ledger.
    38→- Refund and cancellation processing.
    39→- Analytics and reporting.
    40→- Admin configuration and audit logs.
    41→
    42→Do not disrupt existing checkout, payment, inventory, wallet, seller settlement or refund functionality.

   157→# 3. PRODUCT STRUCTURE — AFFILIATE HUB
   158→
   159→Create a dedicated Affiliate Hub for users who participate in the affiliate programme.
   160→
   161→An affiliate is an ordinary NOMOFEE user. Affiliate status must not require seller status or store ownership.
   162→
   163→The Affiliate Hub should have the following sections.
   164→
   165→### A. Affiliate Overview
   166→
   167→Display:
   168→
   169→- Total product-link clicks.
   170→- Estimated unique visitors, where supported by reliable tracking.
   171→- Product views attributed to the affiliate.
   172→- Add-to-cart activity, where available.
   173→- Total attributed orders.
   174→- Paid attributed orders.
   175→- Eligible merchandise sales.
   176→- Personal affiliate commission.
   177→- Sponsor/referral commission.
   178→- Pending commission.
   179→- Approved commission.
   180→- Available commission.
   181→- Paid commission.
   182→- Reversed commission.
   183→- Direct referral count.
   184→- Activated direct affiliates.
   185→- Direct affiliates generating eligible sales.
   186→- Monthly referral-generated sales.
   187→- Date-filtered performance charts.
   188→
   189→Separate the following earnings sources throughout the application:
   190→
   191→1. Personal product promotion earnings.
   192→2. Direct sponsor/referral earnings.
   193→
   194→Never combine these into one unexplained commission figure.
   195→
   196→Clearly label estimated analytics versus authoritative paid-order and financial figures.
   197→
   198→Historical traffic and affiliate data must begin from actual tracking release. Never fabricate or backfill activity that was not historically collected.

   200→### B. Discover Products
   201→

   207→- Product category.
   208→- Product name.
   209→- Seller/store.
   210→- Price.
   211→- Affiliate commission percentage.
   212→- Estimated commission amount.
   213→- Popularity or sales, where genuine data is available.
   214→- Newly added products.
   215→- Featured products.
   216→- Products with attractive commission rates.
   217→
   218→Each product card should display:
   219→
   220→- Product image.
   221→- Product name.
   222→- Seller/store.
   223→- Price.
   224→- Affiliate eligibility.
   225→- Commission rate.
   226→- Estimated earnings for an example qualifying sale.
   227→
   228→Do not display a commission opportunity for products that are not enrolled in the seller affiliate programme.
   229→
   230→Provide an easy action to generate or copy a promotional link.
   231→
   232→### C. My Promotional Links
   233→
   234→Allow affiliates to create, manage, copy and track promotional links.
   235→
   236→Support these link types:
   237→
   238→**1. Product link**
   239→- Promotes one specific product.
   240→- Commission attribution is limited to that product.
   241→
   242→**2. Store link**
   243→- Promotes an individual seller storefront.
   244→- Commission attribution applies only to eligible products belonging to that store.
   245→
   246→**3. Curated collection link**
   247→- Promotes a collection created by the affiliate.
   248→- Commission attribution applies only to products included in the eligible collection scope.
   249→- Consider versioning or snapshotting collection membership at the relevant attribution point so that later collection edits cannot improperly alter past attribution.
   250→
   251→**4. Affiliate showcase link**
   252→- Opens the affiliate's public recommendation page.
   253→- It is not a seller store.
   254→- Products subsequently selected or clicked by the visitor should receive attribution only within the valid showcase attribution scope.
   255→- Do not attribute unrelated purchases across the entire marketplace merely because someone opened an affiliate profile.
   256→
   257→Every link should contain a secure affiliate identifier or signed token.
   258→
   259→Support tracking labels for different external channels, for example:
   260→- TikTok.
   261→- Threads.
   262→- Facebook.
   263→- Telegram.
   264→- WhatsApp.
   265→- Blog.
   266→- Website.
   267→- Other.
   268→
   269→Channel labels are for reporting and do not change the commission split.
   270→
   271→Provide:
   272→- Copy-link action.
   273→- Generate-new-link action.
   274→- Link performance.
   275→- Link status.
   276→- Archive/deactivate where appropriate.
   277→- QR code generation if straightforward with existing dependencies.
   278→
   279→Do not require the affiliate to connect their social accounts.
   280→
   281→Do not claim that NOMOFEE is integrated with TikTok, Meta, Telegram or other external platforms unless a real integration exists.
   282→
   283→### D. My Collections
   284→
   285→Allow affiliates to create curated product collections.
   286→
   287→Examples:
   288→- Best Home Essentials.
   289→- My Favourite Gadgets.
   290→- Recommended Skincare.
   291→- Budget-Friendly Products.
   292→- Raya Shopping Recommendations.
   293→
   294→Features:
   295→- Create collection.
   296→- Edit title and description.
   297→- Add/remove eligible products.
   298→- Reorder products.
   299→- Preview.
   300→- Publish/unpublish.
   301→- Generate shareable link.
   302→- Archive collection.
   303→
   304→A collection is promotional content, not a seller catalogue.
   305→
   306→Only products that are currently eligible for affiliate promotion should generate new affiliate commissions.
   307→
   308→Preserve historical order attribution if a product later becomes unavailable or is removed from a collection.
   309→
   310→### E. My Public Showcase
   311→
   312→Create an optional public affiliate page with a friendly URL, for example:
   313→
   314→`nomofee.my/@username`

   317→- Affiliate display name.
   318→- Profile image, subject to existing privacy rules.
   319→- Short biography.
   320→- Published collections.
   321→- Recommended products.
   322→- Affiliate promotional links.
   323→
   324→It must clearly appear as an affiliate recommendation page, not an official seller store.
   325→
   326→Only expose information the affiliate has explicitly made public.
   327→
   328→Provide appropriate report, moderation and abuse controls.

   331→

   333→

   335→

   339→- Activated affiliates.
   340→- Affiliates who have generated eligible sales.
   341→- Direct sponsor commission earned.
   342→- Monthly performance.
   343→- Referral conversion statistics.
   344→
   345→Where privacy permits, show a limited affiliate identity such as username or display name.

   347→Do not expose email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, payout information or other personal data.
   348→
   349→Do not construct a recursive downline commission tree.
   350→
   351→An affiliate should not see second-generation members as commission-earning members of their network.
   352→
   353→If an affiliate B refers D, D belongs directly to B. A must never receive commission from D's sales.

   355→### G. Earnings

   364→- Commission status.
   365→

   369→- Relevant product/order reference.
   370→- Commission status.
   371→
   372→Display:
   373→- Pending.
   374→- Approved.
   375→- Available.
   376→- Paid.

   391→- Processing status.
   392→- Payout history.
   393→- PSP/provider reference where applicable.
   394→- Failed payout and retry status.

   404→# 4. ONE-LEVEL SPONSOR REFERRAL STRUCTURE
   405→
   406→This section is fundamental. Implement the business rules exactly.
   407→
   408→The referral network can grow without a predefined limit, but commission attribution travels up by only one generation.
   409→
   410→Example:
   411→
   412→Affiliate A refers B and C.
   413→
   414→Affiliate B refers D and E.
   415→
   416→Affiliate C refers F.
   417→
   418→Relationships:
   419→
   420→- B's sponsor is A.
   421→- C's sponsor is A.
   422→- D's sponsor is B.
   423→- E's sponsor is B.
   424→- F's sponsor is C.
   425→
   426→Commission examples:
   427→
   428→- B's eligible product sales: B receives 90%, A receives 10%.
   429→- C's eligible product sales: C receives 90%, A receives 10%.
   430→- D's eligible product sales: D receives 90%, B receives 10%.
   431→- E's eligible product sales: E receives 90%, B receives 10%.
   432→- F's eligible product sales: F receives 90%, C receives 10%.
   433→
   434→A receives nothing from D, E or F.
   435→
   436→Never create a second-generation or deeper sponsor override.
   437→
   438→### Sponsor relationship rules
   439→
   440→1. An affiliate may have zero or one direct sponsor.
   441→2. A sponsor may have unlimited direct referrals.
   442→3. A sponsor must be an eligible NOMOFEE affiliate when the referral is activated.
   443→4. A sponsor cannot refer themselves.
   444→5. Cycles and self-referential relationships are impossible by design.
   445→6. Existing sponsor relationships cannot be casually modified.
   446→7. Affiliates cannot purchase, sell, transfer or trade referral relationships.
   447→8. Administrative correction is allowed only with authorised audit logging and documented reason.
   448→9. An affiliate's inactivity or lack of personal product sales does not automatically remove their sponsor entitlement.
   449→10. No recruitment-only bonus exists.
   450→
   451→The database must enforce the one-sponsor-per-affiliate rule with appropriate foreign keys and uniqueness constraints.
   452→
   453→Do not rely on UI validation alone.
   454→
   455→### Sponsor relationship lifecycle
   456→
   457→A sponsor should be eligible to receive the override only under the programme's documented eligibility rules.
   458→
   459→Proposed policy:
   460→
   461→- Sponsor is approved and active when the referred affiliate activates.
   462→- Sponsor relationship remains recorded permanently.
   463→- No personal sales or login requirement is imposed on the sponsor.
   464→- If the sponsor's payout verification is incomplete, hold their commission until verified.
   465→- If the sponsor is temporarily suspended for suspected fraud or compliance review, hold the sponsor's 10% separately.
   466→- A legitimate direct promoter's 90% should not automatically be penalised because their sponsor is under investigation.
   467→- If a sponsor voluntarily leaves or is permanently disqualified before a future sale, do not transfer their entitlement to another upline.
   468→- Any unallocated sponsor share must remain unallocated and be treated according to the agreed seller-funded commission policy. Never silently increase another beneficiary's entitlement.
   469→
   470→Document these rules in the affiliate terms and financial definitions.

   474→# 5. AFFILIATE REGISTRATION ATTRIBUTION
   475→
   476→This is different from product-sale attribution.
   477→
   478→The purpose is to establish who introduced a new affiliate to NOMOFEE.
   479→
   480→### Referral registration link
   481→
   482→Each approved affiliate receives a unique registration link, for example:
   483→
   484→`nomofee.my/join?ref=AFFILIATE123`
   485→
   486→Support:
   487→- Copy link.
   488→- Share link.
   489→- QR code if appropriate.
   490→- Channel tracking parameters.
   491→- Registration conversion reporting.
   492→
   493→### Proposed registration attribution policy
   494→
   495→Use **first valid referral touch wins** for sponsor attribution.
   496→
   497→Example:
   498→
   499→- New user opens A's valid referral link.
   500→- Later, the same user opens B's referral link.
   501→- A remains the recorded sponsor if A's valid referral touch has not expired.
   502→
   503→Recommended attribution window: 30 days.
   504→
   505→The referral click does not immediately create an affiliate relationship.
   506→
   507→The relationship should only be recorded when the new user completes the appropriate affiliate activation flow.
   508→
   509→### Existing NOMOFEE users
   510→
   511→A registered buyer who has never joined the affiliate programme should be allowed to activate as an affiliate.
   512→
   513→If they have a valid sponsor referral touch, use it.
   514→
   515→If no referral touch exists, allow an optional one-time valid referral code entry during their first affiliate activation, subject to validation.
   516→
   517→An already-active affiliate must not be able to attach a sponsor retrospectively.
   518→
   519→### Sponsor assignment confirmation
   520→
   521→Before activation, show:
   522→
   523→"You were invited by @username."
   524→
   525→Explain that this creates a permanent direct sponsor relationship under the programme rules.
   526→
   527→The user must confirm before activation.
   528→
   529→After activation:
   530→- Sponsor relationship is permanently locked.
   531→- No subsequent referral click can overwrite it.
   532→- No automatic reassignment.
   533→- No seller or admin tool can casually change it.
   534→
   535→### Fraud and account integrity
   536→
   537→Implement:
   538→- Unique account validation.
   539→- Email/phone verification using existing mechanisms.
   540→- Registration rate limiting.
   541→- Bot and suspicious activity detection.
   542→- Self-referral detection.
   543→- Duplicate account investigation.
   544→- Referral abuse monitoring.
   545→
   546→Do not automatically ban users only because multiple accounts share an IP address.
   547→
   548→Families, offices, universities and public networks can share IP addresses.
   549→
   550→Avoid invasive device fingerprinting unless explicitly justified, proportionate and reviewed for privacy and legal compliance.
   551→
   552→Do not reward clicks or registrations.

   556→# 6. PRODUCT-SALE ATTRIBUTION
   557→
   558→This is a separate mechanism from affiliate registration attribution.
   559→
   560→It determines who receives credit for an eligible customer purchase.
   561→
   562→### Proposed product attribution policy
   563→
   564→Use:
   565→- Last eligible affiliate click.
   566→- 30-day attribution window.
   567→- First-party tracking.
   568→- Server-validated affiliate tokens.
   569→- Product-level attribution snapshots.
   570→
   571→Example:
   572→
   573→1. Customer clicks Affiliate B's product link.
   574→2. Customer later clicks Affiliate D's valid link for the same product.
   575→3. Customer purchases.
   576→4. D receives direct attribution.
   577→5. D's direct sponsor receives the 10% sponsor allocation.
   578→
   579→B receives nothing from that sale.
   580→
   581→### Attribution scope
   582→
   583→Product link:
   584→- Only the specified product.
   585→
   586→Store link:
   587→- Eligible products belonging to that store.
   588→
   589→Collection link:
   590→- Products within the collection's validated attribution scope.
   591→
   592→Affiliate showcase:
   593→- Only eligible products selected or clicked within the showcase flow.
   594→- No automatic attribution to unrelated marketplace purchases.
   595→
   596→### Multiple products and multiple sellers
   597→
   598→NOMOFEE supports a single checkout containing products from different stores.
   599→
   600→Therefore:
   601→
   602→**Affiliate attribution must be captured independently for each OrderItem.**
   603→
   604→Example:
   605→
   606→| OrderItem | Product | Seller | Attributed affiliate | Sponsor |
   607→|---|---|---|---|---|
   608→| 1 | Product A | Seller 1 | Affiliate B | Affiliate A |
   609→| 2 | Product B | Seller 2 | Affiliate D | Affiliate C |
   610→| 3 | Product C | Seller 3 | None | None |
   611→
   612→Each item must be evaluated independently.
   613→
   614→One checkout may therefore contain:
   615→- One affiliate-attributed item.
   616→- Multiple items attributed to different affiliates.
   617→- Items with no affiliate attribution.
   618→- Items from different sellers with different commission rates.
   619→
   620→Never assign one affiliate to the entire checkout merely because one product link was clicked.
   621→
   622→### Attribution storage
   623→
   624→Use a secure first-party attribution mechanism.
   625→
   626→Inspect existing cookie and analytics infrastructure before creating anything.
   627→
   628→Use server-side validation, tamper-resistant tokens and reasonable retention.
   629→
   630→Do not trust client-supplied affiliate IDs during checkout.
   631→
   632→The final attribution snapshot must be validated and written server-side.
   633→
   634→A later click for another product must not incorrectly overwrite attribution for an unrelated product.
   635→
   636→A later eligible affiliate click for the same product should follow the documented last-click rule.
   637→
   638→### SellerCampaign separation
   639→
   640→NOMOFEE already has SellerCampaign, UTM tracking and marketing attribution infrastructure.
   641→
   642→Affiliate attribution is a separate commercial mechanism.
   643→
   644→Do not:
   645→- Use SellerCampaign as the affiliate commission ledger.
   646→- Convert all UTM campaign visits into affiliate commission attribution.
   647→- Let an affiliate referral code overwrite unrelated seller marketing attribution.
   648→- Duplicate existing analytics functionality unnecessarily.
   649→
   650→Campaign attribution and affiliate attribution may coexist on the same customer journey, but must remain independently defined and reported.

   654→# 7. SELLER AFFILIATE PROGRAMME
   655→
   656→Sellers must control which products participate.
   657→
   658→Build seller-side configuration into the existing Seller Centre.
   659→
   660→### Seller programme controls
   661→
   662→Allow sellers to:
   663→
   664→- Enable or disable participation in the affiliate programme.
   665→- Set a default affiliate commission rate, if supported by the product model.
   666→- Set individual product commission rates.
   667→- Override the default rate at product level.
   668→- Review participating products.
   669→- Pause individual products.
   670→- Resume previously participating products.
   671→- View affiliate-generated traffic and sales.
   672→- View affiliate commission liabilities.
   673→- View refunds and reversals affecting commission.
   674→
   675→The platform must enforce configured minimum/maximum rate constraints if such controls are introduced.
   676→
   677→Inspect current financial precision and rate conventions before choosing the storage representation.
   678→
   679→Prefer established integer basis-point or equivalent fixed-precision arithmetic.
   680→
   681→Never use floating-point money calculations.
   682→
   683→### Commission changes
   684→
   685→Commission rate changes apply to future eligible orders only.
   686→
   687→Every order item must retain the commission rate and calculation applicable when the sale was created/confirmed according to the existing payment lifecycle.
   688→
   689→Historic orders must never be recalculated using a seller's new commission setting.
   690→
   691→### Seller-facing reports
   692→
   693→Show:
   694→- Participating products.
   695→- Commission rates.
   696→- Attributed product clicks and views.
   697→- Eligible paid orders.
   698→- Commissionable merchandise value.
   699→- Total affiliate pool.
   700→- Direct promoter allocation.
   701→- Sponsor allocation.
   702→- Refund/reversal amounts.
   703→- Current affiliate liabilities.
   704→
   705→Do not expose another seller's affiliate information or confidential customer data.
   706→
   707→### Seller-funded commission
   708→
   709→The seller funds the commission pool.
   710→
   711→NOMOFEE must not accidentally pay seller-funded affiliate commissions from platform operating revenue.
   712→
   713→Inspect existing seller settlement logic and establish how commission liabilities are reserved or deducted from seller proceeds.
   714→
   715→The pool must be accounted for before the corresponding seller funds become freely available for withdrawal.
   716→
   717→Do not create an accounting entry that makes affiliate commission appear as platform revenue.

   720→
   721→# 8. COMMISSION CALCULATION — FIXED 90/10 SPLIT
   722→
   723→The 90/10 split applies to the affiliate commission pool, not directly to the product selling price.
   724→
   725→Example:
   726→
   727→Product commissionable value: RM100
   728→
   729→Seller commission rate: 10%
   730→
   731→Total commission pool: RM10
   732→
   733→Direct affiliate: RM9
   734→
   735→Immediate sponsor: RM1
   736→
   737→### Formula
   738→
   739→Let:
   740→
   741→- B = eligible merchandise commissionable base in minor currency units.
   742→- R = seller's snapshotted commission rate.
   743→- P = total commission pool.
   744→- D = direct affiliate allocation.
   745→- S = immediate sponsor allocation.
   746→
   747→Calculate:
   748→
   749→P = B × R, using the platform's established fixed-precision rate convention.
   750→
   751→If an eligible sponsor exists:
   752→
   753→S = floor(P × 10 / 100)
   754→
   755→D = P - S
   756→
   757→If no sponsor exists:
   758→
   759→D = P
   760→
   761→S = 0
   762→
   763→This rounding rule ensures:
   764→
   765→D + S = P
   766→
   767→and prevents the allocation from exceeding the original pool.
   768→
   769→Use integer sen for MYR and the established currency minor-unit rules for any future supported currency.
   770→
   771→### Commissionable base
   772→
   773→The default intended base is eligible merchandise value after applicable seller-funded discounts, excluding:
   774→- Shipping.
   775→- Taxes.
   776→- Non-merchandise fees.
   777→
   778→However, inspect the actual order and seller settlement snapshots to determine how platform-funded discounts, seller-funded discounts, vouchers and other adjustments affect seller-recognised merchandise value.
   779→
   780→Use the actual authoritative amount that the seller is credited for.
   781→
   782→If the existing model cannot correctly distinguish these amounts, document the limitation and do not invent a commission base.
   783→
   784→### Commission snapshot
   785→
   786→At the appropriate checkout/payment transaction boundary, freeze:
   787→
   788→- Order ID.
   789→- OrderItem ID.
   790→- Store ID.
   791→- Product ID.
   792→- Direct affiliate ID.
   793→- Sponsor affiliate ID.
   794→- Attribution source/link ID.
   795→- Commission rate.
   796→- Commissionable base.
   797→- Total commission pool.
   798→- Direct affiliate amount.
   799→- Sponsor amount.
   800→- Currency.
   801→- Rounding information.
   802→- Relevant timestamps.
   803→- Attribution policy/version.
   804→
   805→Never recalculate an existing order's commission using today's rates or sponsor relationships.
   806→
   807→### No sponsor
   808→
   809→If the affiliate has no sponsor recorded, the direct affiliate receives 100% of the pool.
   810→
   811→### Sponsor temporarily ineligible
   812→
   813→If the sponsor's account is undergoing a temporary verification or risk review, hold the sponsor's 10% separately.
   814→
   815→Do not automatically redirect this amount to the direct affiliate.
   816→
   817→If the sponsor has permanently left or is disqualified before a future sale, use the documented unallocated-share policy.

   885→# 10. COMMISSION RELEASE POLICY
   886→
   887→Use the following lifecycle:
   888→
   889→Pending → Approved → Available → Paid
   890→
   891→Reversed or Held may apply when required.
   892→
   893→### Pending
   894→
   895→A genuine eligible order payment has been confirmed.
   896→
   897→The commission allocation is created and accounted for.
   898→
   899→The corresponding seller-funded commission liability must be reserved or deducted appropriately under the existing settlement model.
   900→
   901→Pending does not mean immediately withdrawable.
   902→
   903→### Approved
   904→
   905→The relevant order item has reached an authoritative fulfilment/completion state.
   906→
   907→Inspect the actual existing order lifecycle.
   908→
   909→Do not invent a delivery timestamp or assume an order has been delivered simply because a certain number of days passed.
   910→
   911→### Available
   912→
   913→The applicable return/refund review period has elapsed, relevant disputes are resolved, and the commission has passed all release checks.
   914→
   915→### Paid
   916→
   917→The payout has actually been processed and reconciled.
   918→
   919→Record the payout reference and actual completion status.
   920→
   921→### COD
   922→
   923→If cash on delivery is supported, do not approve commission merely because the order was created.
   924→
   925→Require an authoritative successful collection/payment confirmation.
   926→
   927→### Existing operational limitation
   928→
   929→Previous Seller Insights work identified that trustworthy carrier delivery timestamps were not available at that time.
   930→
   931→Re-audit the current system.
   932→
   933→If reliable fulfilment evidence is still unavailable:
   934→- Do not invent it.
   935→- Do not automatically release commission on a guessed schedule.
   936→- Keep affected commission pending or under controlled review.
   937→- Document the missing integration or event required for release.
   938→
   939→Admin must be able to understand why an amount is still pending.

   943→# 11. REFUNDS, CANCELLATIONS AND CHARGEBACKS
   944→
   945→Commission reversals must be calculated against the original immutable allocations.
   946→
   947→| Situation | Treatment |
   948→|---|---|
   949→| Payment fails | No commission |
   950→| Order expires unpaid | No commission |
   951→| Full item refund | Reverse full item commission |
   952→| Partial item refund | Reverse affected portion proportionally |
   953→| Shipping-only refund | No product commission reversal |
   954→| Cancellation before eligibility | Reverse pending allocation |
   955→| Chargeback | Hold or reverse affected allocation |
   956→| Duplicate webhook | No duplicate commission or reversal |
   957→| Late refund after payout | Create recoverable adjustment |
   958→
   959→### Partial refunds
   960→
   961→Example:
   962→
   963→Original item commission pool: RM10
   964→
   965→Direct affiliate: RM9
   966→
   967→Sponsor: RM1
   968→
   969→If 50% of the eligible merchandise value is refunded:
   970→
   971→- Direct affiliate reversal: RM4.50.
   972→- Sponsor reversal: RM0.50.
   973→
   974→Use the original allocation snapshot and authoritative refund amounts.
   975→
   976→If the refund model cannot accurately identify which product or amount was refunded, do not create a guessed item-level reversal.
   977→
   978→Hold the affected amount for reconciliation or follow the established manual adjustment process.
   979→
   980→### Late refunds
   981→
   982→If the commission has already been paid:
   983→- Create a documented negative/recovery entry.
   984→- Offset future earnings where permitted by the affiliate agreement.
   985→- Provide a visible explanation.
   986→- Preserve the original payout and commission history.
   987→
   988→Do not silently debit an affiliate's bank account.
   989→
   990→Any recovery process must be reviewed against the applicable affiliate terms and law.
   991→
   992→### Sponsor-specific reversals
   993→
   994→If the underlying product sale is invalid, reverse both allocations.
   995→
   996→If only the sponsor is under investigation, hold the sponsor's 10% separately without automatically reversing a legitimate direct promoter's 90%.
