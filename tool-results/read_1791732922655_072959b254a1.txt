   499→## 7. STORE CREATION, OWNERSHIP VERIFICATION AND CLAIMING
   500→
   501→The generated store must be claimable by its rightful owner.
   502→
   503→This is not merely a database flag or a public button. It is an ownership and account-security workflow.
   504→
   505→### 7.1 Store generation states
   506→
   507→Implement a clear store lifecycle suitable for the existing application, including states such as:
   508→
   509→- Generating.
   510→- Draft.
   511→- Pending ownership verification.
   512→- Pending claim.
   513→- Claimed.
   514→- Published.
   515→- Suspended.
   516→- Archived.
   517→
   518→Use a state machine or equivalent controlled transitions so invalid state changes cannot occur.
   519→
   520→A generated store should not become publicly purchasable before the required claim, seller setup and publication checks have passed.
   521→
   522→If the existing platform supports private previews, allow a pending store to be previewed without making it an unauthorised public shop.
   523→
   524→### 7.2 Claim workflow
   525→
   526→Implement the following flow:
   527→
   528→1. The user submits a supported marketplace URL.
   529→2. The importer retrieves the available data.
   530→3. The application creates the draft store and imports its catalogue.
   531→4. The system generates a unique, secure claim process.
   532→5. The rightful seller registers or logs in.
   533→6. The seller completes the ownership-verification method appropriate to the marketplace and available authorisation.
   534→7. The backend verifies the ownership result.
   535→8. The store is securely associated with the verified account.
   536→9. The seller completes any required business, payment, delivery and domain settings.
   537→10. The seller can preview, configure and publish the storefront.
   538→
   539→### 7.3 Ownership must actually be verified
   540→
   541→Do not let anyone claim any Shopee or TikTok store merely by entering a public URL.
   542→
   543→Implement an appropriate verification strategy, prioritising official marketplace seller authorisation where supported.
   544→
   545→If a platform does not expose a suitable official verification method, investigate a legitimate alternative, such as a supported domain-verification or challenge process. Clearly communicate what evidence is required and validate it on the server.
   546→
   547→A marketplace OAuth connection must be bound to the correct shop identity. Do not assume that any successful marketplace login proves ownership of the particular shop being imported.
   548→
   549→Claim links must be:
   550→
   551→- Securely generated.
   552→- Unpredictable.
   553→- Stored safely.
   554→- Time-limited where appropriate.
   555→- Single-use when appropriate.
   556→- Invalidated after successful claiming or expiration.
   557→- Protected against replay and account enumeration.
   558→- Rechecked on the server at the moment of claiming.
   559→
   560→Use database constraints and transactions to prevent two accounts claiming the same store concurrently.
   561→
   562→Implement an audited recovery process for legitimate ownership disputes and abandoned claims.
   563→
   564→### 7.4 Unclaimed stores
   565→
   566→Unclaimed stores must not provide one user with access to another seller's private information.
   567→
