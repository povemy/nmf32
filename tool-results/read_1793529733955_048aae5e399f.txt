   203→## 4. C1.1 — Fix PaymentGroup / Order expiry race
   204→
   205→This is a critical inventory and payment correctness issue.
   206→
   207→The independent audit found a possible stock-resurrection window:
   208→
   209→1. An order is cancelled by the order-level expiry sweep.
   210→2. Its inventory is restored.
   211→3. Its PaymentGroup remains `requires_payment`.
   212→4. A late successful webhook can still claim the PaymentGroup.
   213→5. The cancelled order can transition to paid.
   214→6. The customer may receive an order for inventory already sold to another buyer.
   215→
   216→This must be fixed.
   217→
   218→### Requirements
   219→
   220→Inspect:
   221→
   222→- PaymentGroup lifecycle.
   223→- Order lifecycle.
   224→- `releaseOrderReservation`.
   225→- `/api/orders/expiry/tick`.
   226→- Payment webhook group-claim logic.
   227→- `transitionOrder`.
   228→- Existing late-payment compensation mechanisms.
   229→
   230→Implement a consistent atomic lifecycle.
   231→
   232→Requirements:
   233→
   234→1. The expiry process must coordinate the associated PaymentGroup and orders.
   235→2. The order-level release path must not restock while leaving an eligible payment group claimable.
   236→3. The webhook must verify the current state of every affected order before applying financial effects.
   237→4. A cancelled or expired order must never be resurrected by a late successful webhook.
   238→5. Stock must be restored exactly once.
   239→6. Late successful payment must create the appropriate durable refund or reconciliation obligation.
   240→7. Scheduler and polling paths must share consistent state-transition rules.
   241→8. `/api/orders/expiry/tick` must not remain an unauthenticated privileged maintenance operation. Require the established cron authentication mechanism.
   242→9. Preserve idempotency and InventoryLog records.
   243→
   244→Do not introduce deadlocks through inconsistent row-lock ordering.
   245→
   246→### Required tests
   247→
   248→- Webhook versus expiry race.
   249→- Expiry versus duplicate expiry.
   250→- Late successful payment.
   251→- Two simultaneous expiry workers.
   252→- Multiple-order PaymentGroup.
   253→- Stock restoration exactly once.
   254→- No resurrection of cancelled orders.
   255→- No duplicate ledger postings.
