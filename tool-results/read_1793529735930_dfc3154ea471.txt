   203→   203→## 4. C1.1 — Fix PaymentGroup / Order expiry race
   204→   204→
   205→   205→This is a critical inventory and payment correctness issue.
   206→   206→
   207→   207→The independent audit found a possible stock-resurrection window:
   208→   208→
   209→   209→1. An order is cancelled by the order-level expiry sweep.
   210→   210→2. Its inventory is restored.
   211→   211→3. Its PaymentGroup remains `requires_payment`.
   212→   212→4. A late successful webhook can still claim the PaymentGroup.
   213→   213→5. The cancelled order can transition to paid.
   214→   214→6. The customer may receive an order for inventory already sold to another buyer.
   215→   215→
   216→   216→This must be fixed.
   217→   217→
   218→   218→### Requirements
   219→   219→
   220→   220→Inspect:
   221→   221→
   222→   222→- PaymentGroup lifecycle.
   223→   223→- Order lifecycle.
   224→   224→- `releaseOrderReservation`.
   225→   225→- `/api/orders/expiry/tick`.
   226→   226→- Payment webhook group-claim logic.
   227→   227→- `transitionOrder`.
   228→   228→- Existing late-payment compensation mechanisms.
   229→   229→
   230→   230→Implement a consistent atomic lifecycle.
   231→   231→
   232→   232→Requirements:
   233→   233→
   234→   234→1. The expiry process must coordinate the associated PaymentGroup and orders.
   235→   235→2. The order-level release path must not restock while leaving an eligible payment group claimable.
   236→   236→3. The webhook must verify the current state of every affected order before applying financial effects.
   237→   237→4. A cancelled or expired order must never be resurrected by a late successful webhook.
   238→   238→5. Stock must be restored exactly once.
   239→   239→6. Late successful payment must create the appropriate durable refund or reconciliation obligation.
   240→   240→7. Scheduler and polling paths must share consistent state-transition rules.
   241→   241→8. `/api/orders/expiry/tick` must not remain an unauthenticated privileged maintenance operation. Require the established cron authentication mechanism.
