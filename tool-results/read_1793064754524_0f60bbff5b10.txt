   212→## R.1 The reported wallet invariant failure — root cause [Measured]
   213→
   214→Reported: wallet `cmvizi0ms000zq7niql6b3csg` (Sofia/Lumina Living) cached {avail 0,
   215→pend 95292} vs ledger truth {avail 10764, pend 84528}, totals equal at 95292.
   216→
   217→**Reproduced exactly** from the live DB (read-only) — `2026-10-25-wallet-drift-deep.json`.
   218→
   219→**Root cause [Measured + code-verified]:** time-based hold maturity with an
   220→event-driven-only cache refresh. T1 credits carry `availableAt = paidAt + holdDays`
   221→(7 d measured). A hold matures **by the clock**; nothing triggers a cache refresh at
   222→maturity; the cache (last written 2026-10-23T08:33Z) was proven **correct as-of its own
   223→write** (truth recomputed as-of `wallet.updatedAt` matches it exactly). At
   224→2026-10-24T14:12Z the 10,764 hold matured with no intervening write → classification
   225→drift only (totals equal). **Not** a writer bug: exactly ONE `wallet.update` call site
   226→exists (grep-verified); the ledger itself is balanced (190/190 txns); app formula
   227→cross-validated against independent raw-SQL aggregation; no currency or cross-wallet
   228→contamination. Fleet audit: 6 wallets, 5 match, 1 stale (±10764, combined-total drift 0).
   229→
   230→**Impact [Derived]:** display-cache staleness + invariant telemetry false-alarm. Money
   231→safety unaffected: payout request/approve/batch authorization recomputes `walletTruth`
   232→**transactionally** and never reads the cache (call-graph verified).
