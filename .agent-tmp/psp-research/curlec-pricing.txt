FAQs

What are payment processing charges?

[]

What are payment processing charges?

Payment processing charges are fees charged by payment service providers to merchants for processing electronic payments from their customers.

Who pays payment gateway charges?

[]

Who pays payment gateway charges?

The payment gateway charges are typically paid by the merchant who is receiving the payment.

How much is payment gateway fees in Malaysia?

[]

How much is payment gateway fees in Malaysia?

The Razorpay Curlec Payment Gateway starts at 1.5% for FPX & eWallets and 2.4% for Debit & Credit Card.

Are payment gateways free?

[]

Are payment gateways free?

Razorpay Curlec charges a minimal setup fee of RM99 for the Basic Razorpay Curlec Payment Gateway package.

Which payment gateway has less charges in Malaysia?

[]

Which payment gateway has less charges in Malaysia?

In Malaysia, the payment gateway providers may have different fee structures and pricing plans depending on various factors such as the payment method, transaction amount, currency, and region. Razorpay Curlec is a payment gateway provider that charges lower fees compared to other providers.
