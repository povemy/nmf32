How HitPay Pricing Works

[]

HitPay Malaysia charges 1.2% + RM1 for domestic cards, online and in person, and 1.8% + RM0.40 for FPX. FPX pays out T+1 business day; cards from T+1.
HitPay uses a simple pay-per-transaction pricing model. Businesses can start accepting payments without setup fees or monthly subscription fees and only pay when a payment is successfully processed.

Pricing depends on the payment method used. For example, domestic card payments (both online and in-person) are 1.2% + RM1 per transaction, while international card payments are 3% + RM1 per transaction.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything businesses ask about our pricing

Everything businesses ask about our pricing

Does HitPay charge a monthly fee in Malaysia?

No. HitPay uses a pay-per-transaction pricing model with no setup fees or monthly subscription fees. Businesses only pay when payments are successfully processed.

[]

How much does HitPay charge for card payments in Malaysia?

Domestic cards are 1.2% + RM1 per transaction, whether the payment is online, in person, or a recurring billing charge. International cards are 3% + RM1 per transaction, with an additional 2% on foreign currency transactions.

[]

What payment methods are supported with HitPay in Malaysia?

HitPay supports a wide range of payment methods including cards and local payment options such as Maybank QRPay and other supported payment methods available through the platform.  You can also collect through WhatsApp payment links.

[]

Are there setup fees to start using HitPay?

No. Businesses can sign up and start accepting payments without setup fees or long-term contracts. Fees are only charged when a transaction is processed.

[]

How much do I earn for referring a business?

0.1% of their monthly transaction volume, every month they keep processing on HitPay. Find your link on Refer and Earn.

[]

Which payments count?

All of them: online, in-person (POS and terminal) and recurring payments.

[]

When do I get paid?

On the 1st of each month, for the previous month's volume. Commission is paid to your linked bank account.

[]

Who can refer?

Any active HitPay merchant in Singapore, Malaysia or the Philippines. Your referral link is already in your dashboard, under Refer and Earn. Agencies, developers and consultants can join the partner programme.

[]

My referral signed up without my link. Can I still get credit?

No. The referral has to be made before the business signs up, using your referral link. It can't be added after signup.

[]

How much is FPX on HitPay Malaysia?

FPX is 1.8% + RM0.40 per transaction. There is no setup fee and no monthly fee.

[]

When do FPX and card payouts arrive in Malaysia?

FPX payouts arrive T+1 business day. Card payouts start from T+1 business day. Other non-card methods, such as DuitNow and e-wallets, pay out T+2 business days.
