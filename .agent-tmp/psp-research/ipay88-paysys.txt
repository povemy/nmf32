Delivering Future-Ready Payment Technology

Paysys provides secure payment solutions for kiosks, vending machines, and retail outlets across Malaysia through reliable POS terminals. We offer both Android and non-Android POS terminals, with flexible sales and rental options, alongside comprehensive maintenance support to meet diverse business needs.

Built on PCI-DSS-compliant platforms, our proprietary hardware and software solutions, including Network Access Controllers (NAC), ensure secure, stable, and efficient transaction flows across all touchpoints.

With seamless POS integration and 24/7 nationwide technical support, Paysys enables businesses to streamline payments while enhancing efficiency, scalability, and reliability.
