Updated a month ago

This guide walks you through creating a Billplz account, from individual identity verification to organization setup. The full process takes about 10 minutes, with bank account verification completing within 24 business hours.

Already have a Billplz account and want to register a new organization? See Add a new organization.

Before you begin

You'll need the following to complete registration:

  • A valid MyKad or passport for identity verification
  • Your organization's SSM/ROC/ROB/ROS registration number
  • A bank account registered under your organization's name
  • A working mobile number and email address for OTP verification

Step 1: Start registration

Go to billplz.com and click Sign up.

Step 2: Create your SSO account

Enter your email address, complete the reCAPTCHA, and click Continue. By continuing, you agree to the Billplz User Agreement and Privacy Policy.

On the next screen, check your inbox for a verification code sent to your email address. Enter the OTP code and click Sign Up.

Step 3: Verify your phone number

Enter your mobile number and click Send Code via SMS. Enter the 6-digit code and click Continue.

Step 4: Enter your identification details

Select your identification type (MyKad or Passport). Enter your full name exactly as it appears on your ID, then enter your ID number.

Complete the reCAPTCHA and click Continue.

Enter your name and ID number exactly as they appear on your document. Even small differences (extra spaces, missing hyphens) can cause rejection. See Fix a rejected ID number if you run into issues.

Step 5: Complete e-KYC verification

Review your identification details on the Verify your identity screen. If anything is incorrect, click Edit to update. When ready, click Continue.

If you are on a desktop browser, a QR code appears. Scan it with your mobile device to continue the verification on your phone. Do not close your desktop browser while the verification is in progress.

On your mobile device, follow the CTOS on-screen instructions to photograph your MyKad (front and back) and take a selfie.

Once verification is complete, return to your desktop browser and click Refresh status to continue.

Step 6: Add your organization

Enter your organization's email address. Check your inbox for the verification code, enter it, and click Continue.

This email becomes your organization's contact email for payment notifications.

Step 7: Enter your organization details

Enter the following:

  • Name as per SSM / ROC / Registration: must match your registration certificate exactly, including suffixes like "Sdn Bhd" or "PLT"
  • SSM / ROC / Registration Number
  • Organization Bank Name: select from the dropdown
  • Organization Bank Account Number: must be registered under your organization's name

Complete the reCAPTCHA and click Continue.

Step 8: Wait for bank account verification

Your account is now created. Bank account verification takes up to 24 business hours. You receive an email notification once verification is complete.

Step 9: Access Billplz Dashboard

After completing your registration:

  • Under Connected Apps, select Billplz.
  • Under Organizations, locate your organization and click the redirect (↗) icon.
  • You will be redirected to the Billplz dashboard.

Step 10: Complete organization registration

The Organization Registration page appears. Your email and bank details are pre-filled from the previous steps.

Fill in the Contact Details section:

  • Customer Service Phone: the phone number shown to customers on billing pages
  • Customer Service Email: the email address shown to customers on billing pages and receipts
  • Mobile Number: your mobile number for account notifications
  • Contact Name: your name or the name of the primary contact person

Enter a promo code if you have one (optional). Complete the reCAPTCHA. Review the Billplz User Agreement and Privacy Policy, then click Submit.

Step 11: Complete additional details

Your Billplz dashboard opens. An Additional Details Required modal appears, requesting information for regulatory compliance.

Fill in the following:

  • Business Address: your registered business address (unit, street, city, postal code, state)
  • What best describes your business?: select your business type from the dropdown
  • Business description: briefly describe your products or services, target customers, and business model
  • Business website or social media URL: enter your website or social media page (include http:// or https://)
  • New SSM (12-digits) / ROS Number: enter your 12-digit SSM or ROS registration number
  • Tax Identification Number (TIN): enter your TIN issued by LHDN. You can find it by logging in to mytax.hasil.gov.my, or on your latest income tax assessment notice. See Glossary: TIN for format details.
  • Is your business SST registered?: select Yes or No. If yes, enter your SST number.

Click Submit.

What's next

To accept card or e-wallet payments, apply for activation now; document review takes a few days. See Activate payments for the process and document checklist.

Meanwhile, your account is ready to accept FPX payments. See Collect your first payment to send your first bill.
