What is Billplz?

Billplz Sdn Bhd is a Malaysian payment gateway that helps organizations collect payments through FPX, Wallet, Card, and Instalments. Since 2012, more than 60,000 Malaysian organizations have used Billplz to get paid. Together they have processed more than MYR 30 billion, including over MYR 5.4 billion in 2025 alone.

We build for one market. A team of around 30 in Selangor runs the platform, staying close to the organizations we serve: businesses, NGOs, schools, zakat bodies, and government agencies. Billplz is a certified PayNet system integrator, and independent audits back the platform. The full list is on our security page.

Organizations use Billplz Collection to accept payments and Billplz Payment Order to send them, through the dashboard, API, or plugins. Billplz is part of Qapital, the operating system for Malaysian SMEs and MSMEs. Two products are live under it today: Billplz for payments and Catalog for commerce, from online stores to payment forms.

Payment preferences in Malaysia keep shifting, from FPX to DuitNow QR to Wallet payments. We keep adapting the infrastructure to match. Thirteen years in, there is still more to build.
