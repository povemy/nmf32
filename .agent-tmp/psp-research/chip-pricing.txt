Frequently Asked Questions

How are payment fees calculated?

[]

CHIP charges a per-transaction fee for every successful payment, based on the payment method selected by the customer. Transaction fees are deducted before CHIP settles the net amount to your business bank account.

Do you charge setup or up-front fees?

[]

CHIP does not charge setup, monthly or annual fees.

Are refund fees charged separately?

[]

Refund fees are only applicable for FPX B2C (RM 1.00) and FPX B2B (RM 2.00) payment methods. No additional charge for other payment methods.

Can I accept in-person payments?

[]

Yes. Use CHIP mini to accept in-person payments using DuitNow QR directly from your phone, with no additional hardware required. Simply download the app, sign in to your account and start collecting payments in minutes.

Are there any contracts or long-term commitments?

[]

No. CHIP does not require long-term contracts or commitments to get started.
