Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) 

1. What is a SME Digital Loan?[]

Our SME Digital Loan is a lending product designed specifically for Malaysian SMEs and existing merchants who need additional funding. No collateral needed. Digital lending is an automated digital process encompassing onboarding, disbursement and repayment process to facilitate the whole micro financing journey. 

As one of the eight approved pioneer 'Credit Community' providers under the Ministry of Housing and Local Government (KPKT), we are amongst the first to roll out digital lending services for our merchants in 2022.

2. What does my business get from the SME Digital Loan?[]

Quick and easy financing solutions to meet your business needs.

Loan amount up to RM200,000.

Benefit from flexible loan tenures of 3 to 12 months, ensuring the right financing option for your business.

3. What are the documents required to apply for the SME Digital Loan?[]

A copy of one of the director's NRIC (front and back).

4. Are there any fees or charges involved?[]

Yes, Stamp Duty and an Attestation Fee (1% of the loan limit and subject to SST) apply. These fees are deducted from the loan amount during disbursement.

5. What if I have more questions about the SME Digital Loan?[]

You can reach out to us via email at ADAPTIS-MY-sales@nttdata.com
