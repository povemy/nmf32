Everything businesses ask about payments in Singapore

The questions Singapore business owners ask most when evaluating payment solutions - answered clearly.

How do I get started with HitPay?

To get started with HitPay, please register your interest with our team. Once your business is approved and your free account is created, you can access HitPay's full suite of payment processing tools.

[]

What types of businesses can benefit from HitPay?

HitPay supports a wide variety of businesses, from e-commerce and retail to hospitality, service providers, nonprofits and more. Our flexible online payment and POS solutions are tailored to be versatile and customisable for your business needs.

[]

Is HitPay secure?

Yes, HitPay employs advanced security measures, including encryption and compliance with industry standards including PCI DSS, to ensure the security of your transactions and data.

[]

What payment methods can I accept with HitPay?

HitPay supports various payment methods, including credit and debit cards, digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay), bank transfers, credit card processing, and more. Click here to see the payment methods available in your location.

[]

Can I use HitPay for both online and in-person payments?

Yes, you can use HitPay to process both online and in-person payments. We offer solutions for e-commerce, mobile, point-of-sale, and more.

[]

How do I integrate HitPay with my website or application?

Integrating HitPay with your website or application is simple, with both no-code and developer-friendly options. Using our step-by-step guides and developer docs, you can implement solutions including plugins, payment links, or use our powerful APIs for seamless integration.

[]

Are there any setup fees to use HitPay?

​HitPay does not charge setup or subscription fees to create an account. Only pay per transaction based on your payment method and payment channel. Please refer to our pricing page or contact our team for more details.

[]

Are there monthly fees to use HitPay?

No. HitPay does not charge monthly fees. Transaction fees apply when payments are processed.

[]

Is HitPay legit?

Yes. HitPay is MAS licensed. Fees are published on /sg/pricing.

[]

What are HitPay's fees in Malaysia?

HitPay has no setup or monthly fee in Malaysia; you pay per successful transaction. Current rates for cards, FPX, DuitNow and e-wallets are on hitpayapp.com/my/pricing. FPX pays out T+1 business days, cards T+1 and other non-card methods T+2.
