Updated a month ago

This guide walks you through creating a Billplz sandbox account for testing. Sandbox accounts let you test payment flows, API integrations, and features using dummy data before going live. The entire process takes about 5 minutes.

Before you begin

  • Use dummy information throughout, no real personal or bank details required
  • Sandbox accounts are for testing only and cannot process real payments
  • You'll need access to an email address for OTP verification

Step 1: Start registration

Go to www.billplz-sandbox.com and click Sign up. This redirects you to Billplz SSO for registration.

Step 2: Create your SSO account

Enter any email address (can be a real or test email). Billplz sends a one-time password (OTP) to that email. Enter the OTP and click Continue, then click Create Account.

Step 3: Add a phone number

Enter any dummy phone number and click Send Code. Enter the 6-digit code shown on screen and click Continue.

Step 4: Complete individual information

Enter dummy individual details in the form (name, identity type, ID number). Click Continue.

Step 5: Add organization details

Click Add to begin entering organization information. Fill in dummy organization details including organization name and registration number.

Step 6: Verify organization email

Enter an email address for organization notifications and click Send Code. Check your inbox (or spam folder) for the 6-digit verification code. Enter the code and click Continue.

Step 7: Complete organization registration

Fill in the organization registration form with dummy information:

  • Organization name (any name)
  • Registration number (any number format)
  • Bank account details

For Bank verification, select DUMMY BANK VERIFIED.

Step 8: Access the Billplz dashboard

Your Billplz SSO account is now created. Click Redirect to App to access your dashboard.

Step 9: Authorize access

On the Authorization page, enter billplz in the text field and click Authorize. This grants the Billplz dashboard permission to read your SSO account information.

Step 10: Add customer service details

Enter dummy customer service contact information (phone, email). These appear on your sandbox billing pages and receipts.

Step 11: Complete the Merchant Application Form

After creating your Sandbox account, complete the Merchant Application Form using sample (dummy) information.

Fill in the following details:

  • Business Website: Enter a sample website URL (e.g. https://www.myonlinestore.com).
  • Business Category: Select any business category and business model.
  • Business Address: Enter a sample business address.

Click Submit to complete the application.

Step 12: Complete Additional Details

Your Billplz Sandbox dashboard opens. An Additional Details Required modal appears.

Since this is a Sandbox account, you can use dummy information for all fields.

Fill in the following:

  • Business Address: Enter a sample business address (unit, street, city, postal code, and state).
  • What best describes your business?: Select any business type from the dropdown.
  • Business Description: Enter a brief sample description of your products or services.
  • Business Website or Social Media URL: Enter a sample website or social media URL (e.g. https://www.example.com).
  • New SSM (12-digits) / ROS Number: Enter a dummy 12-digit registration number.
  • Tax Identification Number (TIN): Enter a dummy TIN.
  • Is your business SST registered?: Select Yes or No. If Yes, enter a dummy SST number.

Click Submit to continue.

What's next?

Your sandbox account is ready. You can now test payment collections, API integrations, and webhooks using sandbox API keys. See Get your Secret Key to locate your sandbox credentials.
